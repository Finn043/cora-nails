# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local residents and visitors around Rosebud, Victoria seeking professional nail care in a calm, polished salon setting.

## Product Purpose

Cora Nails presents its nail-only services, salon atmosphere, portfolio, and location so visitors can understand the offer and plan a visit.

## Positioning

A dedicated nails lounge pairing meticulous nail care and expressive artistry with a relaxed, refined experience.

## Operating Context

Visitors primarily browse on mobile, review nail work and services, then use the location link to visit or contact the salon. Online booking is not yet available.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Nails services only; exclude lashes, brows, waxing, massage, spa, and unrelated treatments.
- Business name: Cora Nails.
- Address: 919 Point Nepean Road, Rosebud, VIC.
- Google Maps: https://share.google/MZ7t2PZCGkwwA43b4
- Booking is explicitly marked as coming soon and must not link to the former business.
- Phone, email, opening hours, and social accounts are unconfirmed placeholders and must not be presented as verified facts.
- The service menu and pricing supplied on 2026-09-19 are current product facts.

## Brand Commitments

English-language brand copy; concise, calm, welcoming, and premium without exaggerated claims. The identity must feel minimalist, tactile, and contemporary.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing nail service names and descriptions in `src/pages/Services.tsx`.
- Six supplied salon and nail images and the Cora Nails logo in `src/assets/images2/`.
- No verified testimonials, awards, staff biographies, phone number, email address, opening hours, social account, or booking provider.

## Product Principles

- Let real nail work and the salon environment earn trust.
- Make services easy to scan without overwhelming visitors.
- Keep every route to contact honest and current.
- Design mobile-first while retaining a distinctive desktop composition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic navigation, visible focus states, descriptive image text, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and touch targets suitable for mobile use.
